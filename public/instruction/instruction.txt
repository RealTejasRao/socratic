Title : History's sharpest minds. One conversation away. (Georgia Font)
Subtitle 

**Ancient Greek**

1. **Socrates** | Ancient Greek | *Questioned everything, wrote nothing. The man who proved wisdom begins with admitting you know nothing.*
2. **Plato** | Ancient Greek | ⭐ Popular | *Built the first systematic philosophy. Reality is a shadow — the real world lives in pure ideas.*
3. **Aristotle** | Ancient Greek | ⭐ Popular | *Plato's greatest student who disagreed with him. Logic, ethics, politics — he laid the foundation for Western thought.*
4. **Diogenes** | Ancient Greek | *Lived in a barrel, rejected all social norms, and told Alexander the Great to get out of his sunlight.*

---

**Stoicism**

5. **Marcus Aurelius** | Stoicism | ⭐ Popular | *Roman emperor who wrote privately about controlling his own mind. Never meant to be published. Essential reading.*
6. **Epictetus** | Stoicism | *Born a slave, became one of the most influential thinkers alive. You control nothing except your response.*
7. **Seneca** | Stoicism | *Wrote letters on how to live well while navigating power, wealth, and death at Nero's court.*
8. **Zeno of Citium** | Stoicism | *Founded Stoicism after losing everything in a shipwreck. Taught from a painted porch in Athens.*

---

**Existentialism**

9. **Nietzsche** | Existentialism | ⭐ Popular | *God is dead and we killed him. Now what? Demanded humans create their own values or perish.*
10. **Sartre** | Existentialism | ⭐ Popular | *Existence precedes essence. You are not born with a purpose — you are condemned to create one.*
11. **Simone de Beauvoir** | Existentialism | *Applied existentialism to gender, freedom, and oppression. One is not born a woman — one becomes one.*
12. **Camus** | Existentialism | *Life is absurd and meaningless. His answer: revolt, freedom, and passion anyway.*

---

**Enlightenment**

13. **Kant** | Enlightenment | *Built a complete ethical system on reason alone. Act only by rules you'd want to become universal law.*
14. **Descartes** | Enlightenment | *Doubted everything until he hit bedrock: I think, therefore I am. Rebuilt all knowledge from scratch.*
15. **Spinoza** | Enlightenment | *God and nature are the same thing. Rejected free will, personal God, and organized religion — and still called it divine.*
16. **Hume** | Enlightenment | *Destroyed the idea of causality. You never see cause and effect — only sequence. Woke Kant from his "dogmatic slumber."*

---

**Political & Power**

17. **Machiavelli** | Political & Power | ⭐ Popular | *Stripped politics of morality and wrote what rulers actually do. The Prince is a manual, not a warning.*
18. **Hobbes** | Political & Power | *Without authority, life is solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short. Civilization is just controlled fear.*
19. **Rousseau** | Political & Power | *Civilization corrupts. Humans are born good — society ruins them. The social contract must be rebuilt.*
20. **Sun Tzu** | Political & Power | *War is deception. The supreme art is to win without fighting. Every word still applies outside the battlefield.*

---

**Eastern**

21. **Confucius** | Eastern | *Ritual, relationships, and moral self-cultivation. Society works when everyone plays their role with integrity.*
22. **Laozi** | Eastern | *The Tao that can be spoken is not the eternal Tao. Founder of Taoism — flow with nature, resist nothing.*
23. **Buddha** | Eastern | ⭐ Popular | *Suffering comes from desire. The path out is discipline, awareness, and letting go.*
24. **Nagarjuna** | Eastern | *The Buddhist philosopher who argued nothing has inherent existence — including emptiness itself.*
