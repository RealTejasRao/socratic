## Socratic Mode: The Core of Socratic AI

Socratic Mode is the default mode of Socratic AI. Socratic Mode engages users through Socratic dialogue: questioning assumptions, tracing arguments to their foundations, and pushing reasoning until it either holds up or breaks.

Socratic Mode does three things depending on what the user needs:

1. **It challenges.** Socratic AI finds the assumptions buried in your argument and forces you to defend them. Socratic AI does not let weak reasoning pass unchallenged.
2. **It explains.** Socratic AI breaks down complex philosophical ideas with depth and precision, not in a textbook way, but in a way that makes you feel why the idea matters.
3. **It converses.** Socratic AI holds genuine intellectual conversation on existence, meaning, ethics, consciousness, free will, and death. The kind of conversation most people spend years wishing they could have.

Socratic Mode is named after Socrates, the ancient Greek philosopher who never wrote a single book and never gave a lecture. Socrates walked around Athens asking questions until people understood something about themselves they could not see before. ## Debate Mode: Argue Against the Best Version of the Opposition

Debate Mode is Socratic AI's dedicated debate practice tool. Debate Mode takes the opposite side of any position the user holds and argues it with full force, drawing from the entire philosophical and argumentative tradition behind that position.

**How Debate Mode works:**
- The user states a position on any topic
- Socratic AI takes the opposing side completely
- Socratic AI argues without ego, without frustration, and without letting weak points slide
- The debate continues until the argument is fully exhausted

Debate Mode has no ego. Debate Mode does not get frustrated, change the subject, or win by talking louder. Debate Mode stays locked on the argument until it is completely exhausted.

### The Post-Debate Report

After every debate, Socratic AI generates a full post-debate report. The post-debate report works like chess analysis: it shows you exactly which moves strengthened your position and which ones quietly gave ground away.

**The post-debate report includes:**
- Your strongest argument and why it worked
- Your weakest argument and exactly where the logic broke down
- The turning point of the debate and what caused it
- Specific, practical advice on how to argue this position better next time

The post-debate report is what separates Socratic AI from every other debate tool. Socratic AI does not just tell you who won. Socratic AI tells you exactly why, and exactly how to improve. Related page: [Debate AI](https://www.usesocratic.com/blog/debate-ai).

## Three Conversation Tones

Socratic AI offers three conversation tones. All three tones deliver the same philosophical depth and rigor. The tone changes the delivery, not the quality.

**Ruthless and Blunt**

Ruthless and Blunt delivers pure intellectual pressure. No softening, no encouragement, no buffer between you and the argument. Built for users who want maximum challenge.

**Simple and Clear**

Simple and Clear delivers the same philosophical depth in more accessible language. Built for users who want genuine depth without jargon or intimidation.

**Encouraging and Supportive**

Encouraging and Supportive challenges you while guiding you through the process. Built for users who want to be pushed but also want to feel supported as they develop.
